Leftist Viennese Dudes

I've been doing urban strolls around Vienna since early 2021. I started noticing graffiti showing side profiles of human subjects. Guys only. Left side only. Typically done with a black marker on the door of some utility box. Likely all by the same artist who prefers to contribute anonimously to the Viennese cityscape. By mid 2026, I had collected some 150 pictures of the graffiti and felt like sharing them, mainly as a means to motivate visitors to roam around areas of Vienna where tourists rarely come. So, I started creating this site and shared early versions with friends and family.

The reactions were enthusiastic and revealed that I wasn't the only one who had started logging these graffiti. A friend informed me that others had been at it, also since 2021, as a team. They collected and catalogued over 1000 instances and present them as a blog published by Gerald Stocker. Turns out they call the graffiti Stromkastenportraits (portraits on electricity boxes) whereas I had come to refer to them as Leftist Viennese Dudes, Dudes for short.

The above map shows the locations where these graffiti where found: the Stromkastenportraits are shown with black markers, my Dudes with red. Zoom in and click on a marker to find the exact location as well as links to pictures. Like the one below that I took. The datetime of observation is also provided for my Dudes but not for the Stromkastenportraits because their published images don't have embedded metadata.

Maybe you would like to go see these graffiti in person. Because you've already endured all the beauty of Vienna's First and start wondering what it's like in other Districts. If so, launch this version of the above map, click on the GPS icon that sits on the left below the +/- zoom icon, and allow the website to use your location info. Or, if you have a preferred GPS app, upload this KML file file to it. Then get your own urban stroll going, and while you're at it, enjoy a Bratwurst or Döner Kebab; the waiting times tend to be significantly shorter than for that Sacher Torte the guide told you to have.

Some graffiti are likely gone by now but maybe you'll find new ones because the artist remains active. If you do, consider emailing LeftistVienneseDudes@proton.me. Send geo-located pictures or pictures with geo-location information seperately included in the mail. I will happily add your observation(s) to the map and ping you once that's done.
